Questions

Frequently asked questions

What owners most often want to know before the first conversation.

How often do you visit my home?

As often as we agree: weekly, every two weeks or once a month. After severe weather I make an additional visit.

How do I know what happened during a visit?

After every visit you receive a short message with photos, by WhatsApp or email. That way you can see the condition of your home for yourself.

Where are my keys kept?

Your keys are stored securely and marked only with a code, never with a name or address. Nobody gains access to your home without your consent.

What happens in an emergency?

I contact you immediately, secure whatever can be secured and agree the next steps with you. Larger expenses are always your decision.

What does the service cost?

That depends on the size of your property, how often I visit and the services you would like. After the viewing you receive a fixed quote with no hidden costs.

Can I book just one service?

Yes. You can book only the checks, only the cleaning or only the arrival service, and add more at any time.

Do you work with my gardener or pool service?

Gladly. I coordinate with the people you already use. If you do not have anyone yet, I can recommend local firms.

Which language do we communicate in?

Whichever is easiest for you. I take care of coordinating with tradespeople and service providers on site.
