Services

Property service

A tradesperson only comes if someone opens the door. A delivery needs someone to receive it. I am that person, and I keep track until the matter is settled.

Scope of service

What is included

  • Secure key holding and key handovers
  • Arranging appointments with tradespeople, opening up, being present and signing off the work
  • Obtaining quotes and summarising them clearly for you
  • Receiving deliveries, furniture and parcels
  • Coordination with gardener and pool service
  • Your contact in an emergency, such as water damage or a storm
  • A report with photos once the work is complete
Process

How it works

You tell me what needs doing. I find the right firms, arrange appointments and am on site while the work is done. You receive a clear report from me: what was done, what it costs and whether anything is still open. Decisions about spending are always yours.

Who it is for

For owners looking for someone on site who takes care of things and keeps them informed.
