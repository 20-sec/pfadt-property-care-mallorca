Services

Arrival and departure service

You land in Palma, drive to Santanyí and unlock the door. The beds are made, the house is aired and the fridge holds what you need for the first evening.

Scope of service

What is included

  • Airing and switching on heating or air conditioning in good time
  • Making the beds and laying out towels
  • Stocking the fridge from your shopping list
  • Preparing the terrace, setting out furniture and cushions
  • After you leave: laundry, emptying the fridge, taking out the rubbish
  • Securing the house: water, electricity, windows, shutters, alarm
  • Storing outdoor furniture and cushions away from the weather
Process

How it works

You send me your travel dates and your wishes, most easily by WhatsApp. I confirm the appointment and let you know as soon as everything is ready. After you leave I put the house into its resting state and send you a short report.

Who it is for

For owners who want to enjoy every hour on the island instead of spending the first and last day on housework.
