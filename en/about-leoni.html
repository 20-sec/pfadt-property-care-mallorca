About Leoni and PFADT Property Care

Passion, trust and reliability in Santanyí.

How it all began

My name is Leoni Pfadt and I have lived in Mallorca since 2023. In that time I have come to love the island and to understand what property owners here need: someone who is there when they cannot be.

Trust as the foundation

Right at the start I was able to take over an existing client base, which has grown steadily since through trust, reliability and recommendations. Today I look after a growing number of properties in the Santanyí area and know each one personally.

How I work

I place great value on a high-quality, reliable service and on transparent communication. That means looking closely, raising what I notice and keeping you informed. You know at all times what is happening in your home.
