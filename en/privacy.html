Data controller

Leoni Enya Pfadt, PFADT Property Care Mallorca, Carrer es Morras 1A, 07659 Cala Figuera, Mallorca, Illes Balears, Spain. For privacy questions, email me at pfadt-propertycaremallorca@outlook.de.

Visiting the website

Hostinger hosts this website. When you visit, Hostinger processes technical access data such as IP address, request time, requested URL, HTTP status and browser identifier. These data help deliver the website, diagnose errors and keep the site secure. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a secure website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Hostinger retains access logs for up to seven days. The website does not use its own cookies or tracking services and loads fonts locally.

Contact by email, phone or WhatsApp

When you contact me, I use your contact details and the information in your message to respond and, where applicable, prepare a quote. The form does not store your entries on the web server. Depending on the option you choose, it opens your email app or passes the prepared text to WhatsApp. You can also contact me directly by email or phone. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform a contract. For other enquiries, my legitimate interest in responding is the legal basis under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Providing your details is voluntary. Without a way to contact you, I cannot reply to you personally.

Recipients and international transfers

Hostinger processes data to provide hosting. I use Microsoft Outlook.com for email. If you choose WhatsApp, WhatsApp Ireland Limited also processes data under its privacy policy. These services may process data outside the European Economic Area. For information about the relevant safeguards, see the notices from Hostinger, Microsoft and WhatsApp.

Retention

I keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and handle any follow-up. If a contract is concluded or statutory retention requirements apply, the relevant periods apply. Where data are needed to establish or defend a claim, they may be kept until the applicable time limit expires.

Your rights

You can request access to your personal data, correction, deletion or restriction of processing. Where the legal conditions are met, you can also request data portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise your rights, email me at the address above. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority such as the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.
